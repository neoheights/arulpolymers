# Product image quality upgrade

## Goal
Make product photography the visual focus across every product catalogue, with clean white image stages, brighter presentation, and the best available sharpness while preserving genuine client products.

## Scope
- Audit all displayed galleries across Automobile, Mining, Potable Water, Aerospace & Defence, Oil & Gas, and ATM Rubber Parts.
- Process the image actually shown by each gallery rather than duplicate source and web copies.
- Preserve every original client file unchanged as the source record.

## Image treatment
- Correct rotation, exposure, white balance, contrast, and restrained sharpening.
- Remove distracting photographic backgrounds and place isolated products on pure white.
- Export new high-quality 4K presentation files on a 3840 × 2160 white canvas, using contain-style placement so no product edge is cropped.
- Keep transparent gaps and soft natural contact shadows where needed to avoid a pasted-on appearance.
- Do not invent product geometry, markings, labels, components, or technical details.
- For extremely small source thumbnails, use conservative enhancement only. Mark these for replacement-source follow-up because true detail cannot be recovered from a 43–225 px source.

## Page presentation
- Point all product galleries to the enhanced permanent website assets, never Drive URLs.
- Standardize gallery stages and thumbnails to pure white with `object-fit: contain`.
- Remove colored scan lines, dark overlays, tinted image-stage backgrounds, and excessive inner spacing over product photography.
- Keep decorative engineering graphics outside the photograph area.
- Preserve current page copy, navigation, anchors, enquiries, and unrelated pages.

## Validation
- Verify every displayed image loads across all product routes.
- Check that each image maps to the correct product and that gallery controls still work.
- Test desktop and 360–430 px mobile layouts for cropping and sideways scrolling.
- Provide a final list of source images too small for genuinely detailed 4K reproduction.
