## Changes
- Keep each existing branded page introduction, then place a simple product navigator directly below it.

- Preserve existing galleries, enlarged viewing, approved details, enquiry links, stable anchors, and enhanced product images.
- Potable Water: remove the oversized selector-card band and special one-off section compositions; use one consistent navigator and three alternating product sections.
- Oil & Gas: move the product navigator above the catalogue, remove the cramped 25/75 sidebar and dark/special product layouts, and render all six products consistently.
- ATM: simplify the page into an Automobile-style introduction, one-item navigator, and one balanced product section; remove duplicated imagery and unrelated role-card presentation.
